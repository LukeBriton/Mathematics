#### Tarski's axiom

[Tarski–Grothendieck set theory - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarski%E2%80%93Grothendieck_set_theory)

If a Tarski universe is [transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitive_set "Transitive set"), it is also a [Grothendieck universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grothendieck_universe "Grothendieck universe").

Conversely, assuming the [axiom of choice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiom_of_choice "Axiom of choice"), every Grothendieck universe is a Tarski universe (i.e. satisfies Tarski's axiom).
