[Affine space - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_space)

An _affine space_ is a set _A_ together with a [vector space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_space "Vector space") $A→\overrightarrow {A}$, and a transitive and free [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_\(mathematics\) "Group action (mathematics)") of the [additive group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Additive_group "Additive group") of $A→\overrightarrow {A}$ on the set _A_. The elements of the affine space _A_ are called _points_. The vector space $A→\overrightarrow {A}$ is said to be _associated_ to the affine space, and its elements are called _vectors_, _translations_, or sometimes _[free vectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_vector "Free vector")_.

$$
V\times E \to E,\quad (v,P)\mapsto P+v
$$
