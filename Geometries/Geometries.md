A pair $(X : G)$, where $X$ is a set and $G$ is a transformation group acting on $X$ will be called a geometry in the sense of Klein.

He enunciated his views on geometries in the framework of what became known as the “Erlangen [ˌɛɐ̯ˈlaŋən] program”.

If one looks at such branches of mathematics as global differential geometry, geometric topology, and differential topology, there appears to be no consensus among the experts about where geometry ends and topology begins in those fields.
