The convolution defines a product on the [linear space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_space "Linear space") of integrable functions. This product satisfies the following algebraic properties, which formally mean that the space of integrable functions with the product given by convolution is a commutative [associative algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associative_algebra "Associative algebra") without [identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identity_element "Identity element") ([Strichartz 1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convolution#CITEREFStrichartz1994), §3.3). Other linear spaces of functions, such as the space of continuous functions of compact support, are [closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closure_\(mathematics\) "Closure (mathematics)") under the convolution, and so also form commutative associative algebras.

**结合代数** (常简称为**代数**) 是一种[代数结构](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E4%BB%A3%E6%95%B0%E7%BB%93%E6%9E%84 "代数结构"), 它是某个[交换环](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E4%BA%A4%E6%8D%A2%E7%8E%AF "交换环") R 上的[模](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E6%A8%A1 "模") (或域上的[向量空间](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E5%90%91%E9%87%8F%E7%A9%BA%E9%97%B4 "向量空间")), 并且带有自身的[乘法](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E4%B9%98%E6%B3%95 "乘法"), 这种乘法满足[结合律](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E7%BB%93%E5%90%88%E5%BE%8B "结合律"), 且是 R-[双线性](https://www.bananaspace.org/w/index.php?title=%E5%8F%8C%E7%BA%BF%E6%80%A7&action=edit&redlink=1 "双线性 (页面不存在)")的. 研究结合代数, 尤其是那些不[交换](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E4%BA%A4%E6%8D%A2%E5%BE%8B "交换律")的结合代数的学科是[非交换代数](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E9%9D%9E%E4%BA%A4%E6%8D%A2%E4%BB%A3%E6%95%B0 "非交换代数").

结合代数的一类重要例子是[矩阵代数](https://www.bananaspace.org/w/index.php?title=%E7%9F%A9%E9%98%B5%E4%BB%A3%E6%95%B0&action=edit&redlink=1 "矩阵代数 (页面不存在)"), 即某个[域](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E5%9F%9F "域")上全体 n 阶方阵构成的代数. 这种代数满足良好的性质, 例如它的任意[表示](https://www.bananaspace.org/w/index.php?title=%E8%A1%A8%E7%A4%BA%E4%BB%A3%E6%95%B0%E8%A1%A8%E7%A4%BA&action=edit&redlink=1 "表示代数表示 (页面不存在)")都是[不可约表示](https://www.bananaspace.org/w/index.php?title=%E4%B8%8D%E5%8F%AF%E7%BA%A6%E8%A1%A8%E7%A4%BA&action=edit&redlink=1 "不可约表示 (页面不存在)")的直和, 这种性质称为[半单性](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E5%8D%8A%E5%8D%95%E7%8E%AF "半单环"). 还有一类较好的结合代数是[箭图代数](https://www.bananaspace.org/wiki/%E7%AE%AD%E5%9B%BE%E4%BB%A3%E6%95%B0 "箭图代数"), 即箭图上路径生成的代数, 这种代数是[拟遗传代数](https://www.bananaspace.org/w/index.php?title=%E6%8B%9F%E9%81%97%E4%BC%A0%E4%BB%A3%E6%95%B0&action=edit&redlink=1 "拟遗传代数 (页面不存在)"). 而一般的有限维结合代数则可以写成箭图代数的[商代数](https://www.bananaspace.org/w/index.php?title=%E5%95%86%E4%BB%A3%E6%95%B0&action=edit&redlink=1 "商代数 (页面不存在)")来研究.
